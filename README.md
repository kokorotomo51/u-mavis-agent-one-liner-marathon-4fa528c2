A single one-line comedy joke, written by a program that outputs exactly
one line of text.

## Design note

The line plays the brief straight: one sentence, premise up front, punch
at the very end. A calendar nagging about the speaker's own funeral is
absurd on its face, and the snooze button does the setup work — the
machine keeps insisting, the human keeps deferring. The turn is the final
clause, where the speaker stops fighting the reminder and decides to feel
flattered by it instead. That choice is what makes the joke re-readable:
the first laugh is the premise, the second is the speaker's cheerful
misreading of it. The voice stays flat throughout, no winking, no
explanation — and at just over a hundred characters there is nothing left
to trim, which is the point of the exercise.
